Safety comes aboard.

NEW — Safety section
• USCG Vessel Safety Check: the fifteen decal requirements plus the recommended items from form ANSC 7012, each carrying the form's own explanation of what is required. N/A is offered only where the form allows it.
• Required Equipment: what federal law requires the boat to carry, by length — PFDs, extinguishers, distress signals, sound signals, lights, ventilation and a backfire arrester.
• Tap Run to work through a list. Tick each item you examine, then answer Yes, No or N/A. Checking and complying are recorded separately, so "I looked and it failed" is a real answer, and an item you never got to stays not-yet-examined rather than failed.
• Every run files three things: a summary in the log with the tally and every finding, an open task for each failure carrying the rule so you know what to put right, and a PDF of the report in Ships Papers under Safety Documents.
• Add your own checklists with the + button and group the steps into sections. Deleted a built-in list? Load brings it back.

NEW — Safety category on equipment and spares
• Tag a record with the requirement it satisfies — Fire Extinguisher, PFD, Visual Distress Signal, Sound Device and more. It then shows when it was last covered by a safety check and how it did, read straight from the latest run. Nothing to keep up to date.
• Flares are as often spares as equipment, so both forms carry the picker.

NEW — NOAA tide predictions
• Tides for the point nearest you now show on the Helm and in the log. No more leaving the app for a tide website.

IMPROVED — the Helm
• Redesigned as one snapshot of the day: where you are, where the boat was last logged, tankage, fuel, engine hours and the estimated fuel left, then today's and tomorrow's weather, then the tides.

IMPROVED — Checklist runs
• A run is now filed under the log for the day it was done. If there is no log for that day, one is created for you, starting from the previous entry's engine hours and position.
• New Maintenance Log filters find Safety Checks and Checklist Runs again.

An inspection performed by someone else — a USCG Auxiliary examiner, a surveyor — is not a run. Scan their form into Ships Papers under Safety Documents with its date and it carries an expiry reminder like any other paper.

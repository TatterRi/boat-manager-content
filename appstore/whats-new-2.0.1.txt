SYNC THAT KEEPS GOING
A vendor used on a shared boat could stop a device uploading, while iCloud Sync still said all was well. That's fixed, and iCloud Sync now says when uploads have stopped and which record is holding them up.

STEADIER FORMS
Log Note no longer crashes on the Mac, and coming back from a list on the expense and log forms keeps what you've typed.

LOG NOTES YOUR WAY
Time and Position are checkmarks on Log Note, remembered for next time, so a poor GPS fix stays out of the log. The log form has a note button by the Narrative, and Diver is among the notes.

SHIPS PAPER ON THE +

VENDORS

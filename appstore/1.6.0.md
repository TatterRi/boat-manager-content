## Review notes (App Review, not public)

Replace only the release-specific first paragraph; the rest are standing
explanations (subscription, RevenueCat, contacts). The macOS version keeps
its "macOS companion" line above it, with the version changed.

    1.6.0 adds facility details to waypoints and a Fill from Maps action that
    looks up a marina with MapKit (MKLocalSearch) and the public OpenStreetMap
    Overpass API; only the waypoint's coordinates are sent, and the user
    chooses what to copy. Maintenance items gain a 500-hour/12-month service
    column (a new boolean attribute, migrated once on launch); the CloudKit
    schema has been deployed to production. Log and task context menus are
    reorganised, and CSV export/import now includes float plans. Purchase and
    restore are unchanged.

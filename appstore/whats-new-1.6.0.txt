Waypoints made for liveaboards, filled in from Maps, and every part of the app a tap closer.

WAYPOINTS FOR LIVEABOARDS
Record your own view of a place: type, operator, address, Safe Harbor (with your member number), VHF, phone and email. At the berth: slips, moorings or anchorage, depth, dockage, shore power by amps and volts, water, fuel, pump-out, wake protection, dinghy dock. For liveaboards: welcome or transients only, mail and packages, gated docks, feels safe, walkable, Uber. Ashore: laundry and its cost, pharmacy, medical, bars and restaurants by name, marine stores, pet friendly, courtesy car. Services: yard work, propane, mechanical, electrical, canvas and more. Each is Yes, No or Not known, dated when it changes.

FILL FROM MAPS
While editing a waypoint, tap Fill from Maps. Apple Maps gives the marina's name, phone and address and what's nearby; OpenStreetMap gives the boating facilities. Tick what to copy; nothing you entered is replaced unless you choose.

FIND THE RIGHT PLACE
Filter waypoints by what they have — moorings, pump-out and liveaboards welcome, together. Each shows how often you've logged there.

NAMES CAN CHANGE
Rename a waypoint and its logbook keeps every visit. Crew get a Display Name for the logs — Niki, not Niki Tattersfield — and a rename carries through their logs, tasks and float plans. Renamed maintenance items keep their history; to-do tags follow what they tag.

ICONS THAT TAKE YOU THERE
Blue icons now do something. On a log row they open the log at that part: Tankage, Maintenance, Weather, the berth, the people aboard. Equipment, Spares and Tools icons open those groups; a document's icon opens the file, an expense's paperclip its invoice, a task's gear its equipment.

A LOGGED PORT BECOMES A WAYPOINT
Tap Save as Waypoint on any log whose port has none. Choosing a port, the list starts with the three nearest.

ONE MENU FOR EVERY LOG
Long-press a log on any list for the same menu: open, copy to another date, add fuel, pump-out, water, propane or maintenance to that day, or delete, which now asks first.

TASKS FROM THE MAINTENANCE LOG
Each task's ⋯ button marks it complete, sets who did it and the equipment, adds a spare, or copies it to another log, without opening it.

SIX MONTHS GETS ITS OWN COLUMN
Service columns are now 50 h or 1 month, 100 or 3, 250 or 6, 500 or 12, 1000 or 24. Your items move by themselves.

EVERY SCREEN AT A GLANCE
Groups start closed and each header shows what's in it. Lists show a photo or icon beside each name.

MORE ROOM ON IPAD AND MAC, QUICKER ON IPHONE
The bottom bar is gone: the lifebuoy sits beside the Helm, iCloud Sync and Settings at the foot of the menu, checklists side by side. iPhone opens on your default boat.

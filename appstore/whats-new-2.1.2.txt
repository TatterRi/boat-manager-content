Log a note with Siri, Canadian tides, sharing a boat every way, and forecasts from Apple Weather and NOAA.

SHARING A BOAT, EVERY WAY
2.1.1 stopped a shared boat's records from halting iCloud uploads. 2.1.2 checks every link each time something is saved, whatever it joins: a paper for a crew member, a job done by one of your own vendors, a to-do logged as a job, a float plan, a category, or anything used on both a shared boat and one that isn't. None of them can stop an upload now, and the first time Boat Manager opens it sends anything that was waiting. iCloud Sync also tells offline from stopped, and says when iCloud's storage is full.

FORECASTS FROM APPLE WEATHER AND NOAA
A voyage's forecast now comes from Apple Weather, 10 days ahead. Wave heights come from NOAA: the National Weather Service forecast in US coastal waters and the Great Lakes, and NOAA's WaveWatch III wave model further offshore. In a marina or harbour, the waves shown are the ones waiting outside. Inland, with no open water near, none show rather than a 0.

LOG A NOTE WITH SIRI
Say “Log a note in Boat Manager” and what you say goes on today's log with the time and, when there is one, the position, without opening the app. It's in Shortcuts and on the Action button too. For the new screens.

CANADA, AND OUTSIDE THE US
Canadian tides, as a guide: 157 places worked out on your device, so they need no signal. They aren't the official tables and aren't for navigation. Set to Canada, weather and tide links open Environment Canada and tides.gc.ca, a float plan's PDF has the Canadian Coast Guard's rescue lines, and forms say Province and Postal Code. Outside the US, a new install starts in metric, the log's weather is in your own °C or °F, and US towing numbers aren't offered away from US waters unless you're a member.

WHERE WE ARE
Show has a new Where We Are section: your latitude and longitude, together and in decimal, each a tap to copy.

LICENSES AND CERTIFICATES
Credentials keep licenses and certificates on each crew card, with expiry reminders. The owner sees everyone's; crew see their own. Your crew need this version too.

SATELLITE MAPS
A button on the new screens' maps switches all of them between standard and satellite.

LONG ICLOUD SYNCS SAY HOW FAR ALONG THEY ARE
When a lot is going up or coming down, iCloud Sync shows how much is done and about how long is left.

ONE OF EACH, EVERYWHERE
Your categories, to-do sections and bodies of water show once wherever you use them, on your own boats and a shared one alike, and renaming one changes it everywhere. A library job you edit changes on the shared boat too. A float plan keeps its skipper's details, so its PDF is complete whoever has the boat. And a rare crash at launch is fixed.

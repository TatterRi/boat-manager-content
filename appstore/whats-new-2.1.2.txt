SHARING A BOAT, EVERY WAY
2.1.1 stopped a shared boat's records from halting iCloud uploads. 2.1.2 covers every other way we found: a to-do tagged with a shared boat, a float plan filed for one, a to-do put in one of your sections, a place given its body of water, a paper added as you save, and an expense category used on both a shared boat and one of your own. None of them can stop an upload now, and the first time Boat Manager opens it sends anything that was waiting.

ONE OF EACH, EVERYWHERE
Your categories, to-do sections and bodies of water show once wherever you use them, on your own boats and a shared one alike, and renaming one changes it everywhere. A library job you edit changes on the shared boat too. A float plan keeps its skipper's details, so its PDF is complete whoever has the boat. And a rare crash at launch is fixed.

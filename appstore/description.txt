Know what your boat really costs, and keep every detail of every boat you run — logbook, maintenance, safety, documents and crew — in one place, on iPhone, iPad and Mac.

Built by a USCG-credentialed Master Mariner and ex-Royal Navy officer, over several thousand miles underway.

EXPENSES & WHAT THE BOAT COSTS
• Every cost an expense of its own: fuel, berths, labour, parts, insurance, subscriptions
• What the boat costs over the last 12 months and what's coming: fixed, semi-fixed and discretionary, with capital kept apart
• Every figure opens the expenses behind it; tap a year for its spending by category
• Recurring bills set up once, then recorded or skipped when due
• Refunds and insurance payouts netted against the cost they repay
• What you've spent at each marina and with each vendor
• Export to PDF or CSV for your taxes or your bookkeeper

COMPREHENSIVE LOGBOOK
• Captain's daily log, fuel log, pump-out log and optional generator log
• Apple Weather conditions and NOAA tides recorded with each entry
• GPS position and waypoints on an embedded map
• Sea service summaries for USCG CG-719S

SAFETY
• The USCG Vessel Safety Check and a federal Required Equipment list, ready to run
• Each run files a report, a task for every failure, and a PDF on the boat's papers
• Tag gear with the requirement it satisfies and see when it was last checked

MAINTENANCE & PLANNING
• Planned jobs by date or engine hours: log, reschedule or skip, with a reminder that follows
• An Outlook of what falls due month by month, budgets included
• Projects: gather a refit or yard visit into one job with dates, budget and progress
• A library of planned-maintenance tasks and intervals, and checklists you build
• Maintenance Dashboard for the equipment you care about

EQUIPMENT, TOOLS & SPARES
• Every piece of equipment on every boat, with its full service history
• Manufacturer, part number, serial number and cost
• Link spares to the equipment they fit
• Warranty and calibration dates, with reminders
• Paint, glue and epoxy tracked from when they're opened

DOCUMENTS & EXPIRY DATES
• Registration, insurance, certificates, manuals, receipts, photos and videos
• Attach files to boats, logs, equipment, spares, tools, crew, waypoints or notes
• Expiry dates for documents, equipment and spares, with a reminder ahead and one-tap renewal

FLOAT PLANS
• USCG-inspired float plans, sent by Messages or Mail in one tap
• One-tap arrival message with your position, which drafts the log entry
• Emergency message with your position

CREW & VENDORS
• Crew lists created from, or kept in step with, Apple Contacts
• See every log entry each crew member was aboard for
• Yards, mechanics and marinas listed by business, with what you've paid them

NAVIGATION & THE HELM
• Import waypoints by GPX or CSV; see where you've been on a calendar and a map
• The Helm: position, tides, weather, engine hours, fuel and what is due
• To-do list, shopping list and notes

ANALYSIS & REPORTS
• Days underway, speeds, distances, fuel and tankage, charted
• Data Health reads your logs for what cannot be right and proposes the fix
• PDF reports for boats, logs, maintenance, equipment, spares and tools

APPLE ECOSYSTEM
• iCloud sync across iPhone, iPad and Mac, with full offline use
• Apple Reminders, Calendar, Contacts and Weather throughout
• Dark Mode for night watches
• CSV import and export of everything, with blank templates for your own spreadsheets

Every detail of every boat you run — logbook, maintenance, safety, documents and crew — in one place, on iPhone, iPad and Mac.

Built by a USCG-credentialed Master Mariner and ex-Royal Navy officer, over several thousand miles underway.

COMPREHENSIVE LOGBOOK
• Captain's daily log, fuel log, pump-out log and optional generator log
• Apple Weather conditions and NOAA tides recorded with each entry
• GPS position and waypoints on an embedded map
• Sea service summaries for USCG CG-719S
• Optionally synced to a shared Apple Calendar

SAFETY
• The USCG Vessel Safety Check and a federal Required Equipment list, ready to run
• Each run files a report, a task for every failure, and a PDF on the boat's papers
• Tag gear with the requirement it satisfies and see when it was last checked
• Build your own safety checklists

MAINTENANCE, PLANNING & EXPENSES
• Planning: an Outlook of what falls due month by month, repeats and budgets included
• Projects: gather a refit or yard visit into one job with dates, budget and progress
• Schedule several jobs in one pass, as tasks, Reminders, or both
• Service intervals dated from your logged engine hours
• A library of planned-maintenance tasks and intervals
• Build your own checklists, by date or by service hours
• Maintenance Dashboard for the equipment you care about
• Expenses log with CSV export and PDF report

EQUIPMENT, TOOLS & SPARES
• Every piece of equipment on every boat
• Full service history from the Maintenance Log
• Manufacturer, part number, serial number and cost
• Link spares to the equipment they fit
• Warranty and calibration dates, with reminders

DOCUMENTS & EXPIRY DATES
• Registration, insurance, certificates, manuals, receipts, photos and videos
• Attach files to boats, logs, equipment, spares, tools, crew, waypoints or notes
• Share a photo straight in as a new ToDo, picture attached
• Expiry dates for documents, equipment and spares, with Calendar reminders

BOAT DETAILS
• Details, specifications and capacities for any or all of your boats
• Fuel consumption curve fitted to your own data, to estimate what is left

FLOAT PLANS
• USCG-inspired float plans created from within the daily log
• Export to PDF or send by Messages or Mail in one tap
• One-tap arrival message with your position, which drafts the log entry
• Emergency message with your position

CREW & CONTACTS
• Crew lists with contact details and default-crew flags
• Created from, or kept in step with, Apple Contacts
• See every log entry each crew member was aboard for

NAVIGATION & VISUALIZATION
• Import waypoints by GPX or CSV
• Calendar and Map views of where you have been, linking back to each log entry
• NOAA tide predictions, and your own nautical-weather link

THE HELM
• A pop-over dashboard: position, tides, weather, engine hours, fuel and what is due
• To-do list, shopping list and notes, with categories you name yourself

ANALYSIS & REPORTS
• Statistics and charts: days underway, speeds, distances, costs, fuel and tankage
• Data Health reads your logs for what cannot be right and proposes the fix
• Carbon-footprint estimate from your fuel use
• PDF reports for boats, logs, maintenance, equipment, spares and tools

APPLE ECOSYSTEM
• iCloud sync across iPhone, iPad and Mac, with full offline use
• Apple Reminders for maintenance and admin dates
• Apple Calendar, Apple Contacts and Apple Weather throughout
• Dark Mode for night watches
• CSV import and export of everything

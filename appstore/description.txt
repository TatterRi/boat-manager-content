Every detail of every boat you run, in one place: the log, upkeep, gear, papers, costs and crew, on iPhone, iPad and Mac.

Built by a former USCG-credentialed Master Mariner and ex-Royal Navy officer, over several thousand miles underway.

NEW IN 2.0
• A simpler set of screens: your boat on its own page, with the Helm, Find, a + for anything new, and Show
• Arrange it your way: the places, the Helm's tiles and the order of your boats
• Share a boat with your crew, free for them
• One search across the log, gear, spares, papers, jobs, people and places
• Voyages planned week by week

YOUR BOATS
• Any number of boats: your main boat, a tender, and the boats you've owned or served on
• Specs, admin details, dimensions and capacities for each
• A fuel-consumption curve fitted to your own data, to estimate what's left in the tank

LOGBOOK
• Captain's daily log, fuel, water, pump-out and optional generator logs
• Position, Apple Weather and NOAA tides filled in with each entry
• Guided Log Entry takes a day's log one step at a time
• Every passage on a map and a calendar; import waypoints by GPX or CSV
• Sea service summaries for USCG CG-719S

THE HELM
• Position, weather, NWS alerts, tides, engine hours, fuel and what is due
• Cards when something needs doing: a gale warning, today's float plan, jobs coming due

CREW
• Share a boat as Writes the Log, Crew or View Only; it comes aboard their Boat Manager through iCloud
• Crew don't pay: your subscription covers your boats
• Each log entry, to-do and expense says who wrote it; crew keep their own time aboard
• Crew lists from Apple Contacts, with every log entry each person was aboard for

VOYAGES
• Plan the coming weeks and months: stops, days between, fuel, weather on arrival and the cost
• Mark a day that can't move, compare alternatives side by side, send the stops to your calendar

UPKEEP & PLANNING
• Over a hundred planned-maintenance jobs, by date or engine hours: log, reschedule or skip
• An Outlook of what falls due month by month, budgets included
• Projects for a refit or haul-out; ready-to-run checklists, and your own

GEAR, SPARES & PAPERS
• Every piece of equipment with its full service history, spares and tools
• Registration, insurance, manuals, receipts, photos and videos, attached where they belong
• Expiry dates with a reminder ahead and one-tap renewal

SAFETY
• The USCG Vessel Safety Check and Required Equipment list, ready to run: a report, a task for every failure and a PDF
• Hurricane Plan and Storm Prep checklists
• USCG-inspired float plans and an arrival message, sent by Messages or Mail in one tap

WHAT THE BOAT COSTS
• Every cost an expense, with its receipt: fuel, berths, labour, parts, insurance
• The last 12 months by category, recurring bills, and spend at each marina and vendor
• PDF or CSV for your taxes or your bookkeeper

WAYPOINTS & ANALYSIS
• Waypoints for liveaboards: berth, power, water, pump-out and what's ashore, filled from Apple Maps
• Days underway, speeds, distances, fuel and carbon footprint, charted
• Data Health finds what cannot be right in your logs and proposes the fix

APPLE ECOSYSTEM
• No account to make: your data stays in your own iCloud, synced across iPhone, iPad and Mac, and works offline
• Apple Reminders, Calendar, Contacts and Weather; CSV import and export of everything
• Dark Mode for night watches

Everything is free for the first 30 days. After that it is $0.99 per month or $9.99 per year, billed through your Apple Account and cancellable at any time.

Note: the emergency and float-plan features are aids, not a substitute for a VHF/DSC radio, an EPIRB, or leaving a float plan with someone ashore.

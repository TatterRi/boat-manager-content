## App Review notes: the 2.1.4 first paragraph

Replaces only the first paragraph; the standing ones stay. Needs Rick's approval.

> 2.1.4 checks engine-hour readings as they are entered and asks before saving one that looks wrong, lets a replaced hour meter restart without counting hours twice, and says about when a job by engine hours falls due, with an optional notification a week ahead. Siri can now log each of the Log Note buttons and log a fishing catch; these App Shortcuts need no set-up. With the device set to the UK or Canada, wording, weather and tide links, rescue numbers and credentials follow the region, and UK tides are worked out on the device as a guide, labelled not for navigation. Voyages show US Coast Guard Notices to Mariners and tides at each stop. Nothing needs set-up.

## Review notes (App Review, not public)

Replace only the release-specific first paragraph; the rest are standing
explanations (subscription, RevenueCat, contacts). The macOS version keeps
its "macOS companion" line above it, with the version changed.

    1.5.2 is a point release. A PDF button on the equipment form renders a
    report of that piece of equipment's record on the device, for the user to
    share; nothing is uploaded. The Helm's fuel card adds fuel left and an
    estimated range, both worked out from the user's own logs. Maintenance
    items gain a 6-month interval, stored in the existing fields: there is no
    data model change and no CloudKit schema change. Filter menus gain icons.
    Purchase and restore are unchanged.

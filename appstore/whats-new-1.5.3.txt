ADDING A LOG WORKS AGAIN
Tapping + on the log list opens the new log entry form again. We think this was caused by a change in iOS 27. Nothing you had already logged was affected.

Everything new in 1.5.2 is here too: the equipment report, fuel left and range on the Helm, the 6-month maintenance interval, and icons on every filter.

### Shorter variant

    RECEIPTS — share a receipt or invoice into Boat Manager and file it as an expense on that day's log, or a yard invoice on its maintenance task.

    SAFETY CHECKS — open one and it answers what your records can: tagged gear, what the boat carries, extinguishers against her length. Expired flares are flagged before you start.

    EQUIPMENT — a task is now linked to the equipment it was done on, so renaming the equipment no longer hides its history.

    Plus Data Health finds duplicates, and the app no longer waits on "Checking subscription" with no signal.

## Review notes (App Review, not public)

    1.5.1 is a point release. The share extension can now file a PDF or photo
    as an expense against a day's log, or against a maintenance task. Safety
    checklists pre-answer items from the boat's own records; nothing is saved
    as checked until the user confirms it. Maintenance tasks are linked to their
    equipment (a new Core Data relationship, synced through CloudKit). The
    subscription check now falls back to the last entitlement seen on the
    device after six seconds with no network, rather than waiting indefinitely;
    purchase and restore are unchanged.

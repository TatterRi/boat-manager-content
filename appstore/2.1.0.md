## App Review notes: the 2.1 first paragraph (draft, 19 Nov)

Replaces only the first paragraph; the standing ones (subscription,
RevenueCat, contacts) stay.

> 2.1 is a maintenance release. Each piece of equipment now keeps its own
> jobs, set up from books: the built-in Starter Book, or Read My Manual,
> which reads the maintenance table in an engine manual's PDF with Apple's
> Foundation Models (Private Cloud Compute, with the on-device model when
> there is no connection) and proposes a book of jobs that the owner reviews
> before anything is saved. To try it on iOS 27 with Apple Intelligence: add
> any engine or generator manual PDF in Papers, then open the engine in Gear
> and choose Add Jobs › Read My Manual. Elsewhere it says what it needs, and
> the Starter Book's jobs are offered instead. The rest of 2.1 needs no set-up:
> Service Checklists, pinned jobs, spares and stock, batteries and power,
> Call for a Tow and Send to a Technician.

## App Review notes: the 2.1.2 first paragraph

Replaces only the first paragraph; the standing ones stay.

> 2.1.2 completes 2.1.1's iCloud fix for shared boats: more kinds of record are kept within a boat's share, so none can stop uploads. Voyage forecasts now come from Apple WeatherKit (Apple's Weather mark and legal link appear wherever forecasts do: a voyage plan's itinerary, a stop, What's Here, voyage info) and wave heights from NOAA's free public data (api.weather.gov; WaveWatch III via PacIOOS ERDDAP), no key or account, only a position sent. Open-Meteo is no longer used. “Log a note in Boat Manager” is an App Shortcut: Siri adds the spoken note to the day's log without opening the app. Canadian tides are worked out on the device from open tidal constants (TICON-4, CC BY 4.0, credited in the app) and labelled a guide, not for navigation. Nothing needs set-up.

## Waves outside and Where We Are (noaa-weather 03a2eb37, 21 Nov)

Rick asked for both on 21 Nov. In a marina or harbour NOAA's grid square reads
0 ft all week (Charleston City Marina), so the Helm hid the waves: a calm square
now looks out in rings of NWS squares (about 5 to 30 km) and shows the biggest of
the first ring with waves, labelled "x ft waves outside" on the Helm; voyage
forecasts use the same lookup. Show gains a Where We Are section (latitude,
longitude, both, decimal; tap to copy). No model, help or privacy-page change: the
extra lookups go to the same api.weather.gov. What's New: the forecasts entry's
last sentence rewritten, and a Where We Are entry added.

## Every link (fix/zone-invariant, 24 Nov)

Rick asked whether any path to a sync freeze was left. A test of every relationship
in the model, in every arrangement of shared and unshared, found 124 of 172 link
ends 2.1.2's guards left able to join two iCloud zones (logging maintenance with an
own vendor, a crew member's paper, a to-do, a category's parent, anything moved
between a shared boat and one that isn't). One last check on every save now leaves
none. Also: Data Health's lookups no longer hold up an upload, three save observers
no longer wait on the main thread, offline isn't shown as stopped, and Download
Again keeps the old copy for two weeks. No model change: no CloudKit deploy.

## What's New in This Version

Paste into App Store Connect → *What's New in This Version*. (Limit 4,000
characters; this draft is 2,453.)

See `whats-new-1.5.0.txt` for the paste-ready text. Entry order follows the
in-app collection, which leads on tides rather than Safety: tides are what a
user sees without doing anything, Safety needs them to go and run a check.

### Shorter variant

If you would rather keep the notes brief:

    TIDES — the Helm reads NOAA predictions for the nearest station: height now, flooding or ebbing, every high and low of the day. Pin a station and it works without a signal.

    SAFETY — a new section beside Maintenance. The USCG Vessel Safety Check and a federal Required Equipment list, ready to run. Each run files a report in the log, a task for every failure, and a PDF on the boat's papers.

    GEAR — tag an extinguisher, flares or a PFD with the requirement it satisfies, and its record shows when it was last checked.

    PHOTOS — share a photo into Boat Manager and file it as a ToDo with the picture attached. ToDos now take a reminder with a due date and repeat.

    DATA HEALTH — in Settings. It reads your logs for what cannot be right, says why it matters, and proposes the fix. Nothing changes until you tap Fix.

    Plus a tidier Helm, a straighter fuel estimate, checklist runs filed under the day they were done, and region-proof CSV exports.

## Review notes (App Review, not public)

    1.5.0 adds a Safety section (USCG Vessel Safety Check and a federal Required
    Equipment list, run in-app, filing a log report, tasks and a PDF), NOAA tide
    predictions on the Helm and in the log, a share extension that files a photo
    as a ToDo, and Data Health in Settings. Tides use NOAA's public CO-OPS
    predictions; no account is required. Subscription behaviour is unchanged from
    the previous release. A default boat is created on first launch, so Safety can
    be reached from a fresh install without entering any data.

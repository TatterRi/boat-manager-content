## App Review notes

Drafted in Boat Manager's `claude/review-notes-2.0.md` (9b52e9f1), 3,706 of
4,000 characters. No demo share and no video (Rick, 1 Nov): the notes say why
crew is optional (shares are private to invited iCloud accounts). The release paragraphs are new and two standing
ones are rewritten; replace the whole of the notes, not only the first
paragraph, this time.

> 2.0 brings a new, simpler set of screens. New installs start on them; existing users keep the previous screens and can switch in Settings › Boat Manager 2.0. A new user chooses "Set up my boat" (a boat, a home port, permissions, then the free month on the subscription screen) or "Joining someone's boat?".
>
> New, crew: an owner can share a boat through CloudKit sharing (CKShare, UICloudSharingController; CKSharingSupported), inviting a person by their iCloud email or phone with a role: Writes the Log, Crew, or View Only. They open the invitation and the boat appears in their app. Records go only to invited people, through iCloud, never a developer server. A shared boat is free; crew never see the subscription screen. Shares are private to invited iCloud accounts, so we can't share a boat to the review device in advance; crew is optional and every other part of the app works without it.
>
> Also new since 1.8.0: Voyages, for trips over weeks and months; a VoyagePlan user's voyages are copied in once from its container (iCloud.com.rtackconsulting.PassagePlanner, hence the entitlement). Weather from Open-Meteo and alerts from api.weather.gov are sent only coordinates. To-dos can sync both ways with a list in Apple Reminders (same permission as before). No new permissions. The CloudKit schema has been deployed to production.
>
> The purpose of the app is for boat owners to record key details about their boat and its upkeep. It requires one boat to work; everything else is optional. Starter content (a maintenance library and safety checklists) is offered during set-up. The app uses Location, MapKit, WeatherKit, EventKit (Reminders and Calendar) and Contacts. The developer has no access to users' data: it is kept on the device and in the user's own iCloud, and shared only with crew the user invites.
>
> A subscription (monthly or annual) is required for boats you own. It is free for the first month. Boats shared with you by their owner are free.
>
> We use RevenueCat to manage purchases. The app creates no accounts of its own. So that one person's iPhone, iPad and Mac count as one customer, RevenueCat's app user ID is the user's CloudKit user record ID (an opaque identifier from CKContainer.userRecordID() that carries no name or email); without an iCloud account it stays RevenueCat's anonymous ID. 'Delete My Account' calls Purchases.logOut(), which resets that ID and clears the session. In sandbox mode, Apple may still associate purchases with the test account.
>
> We added Terms of Use: https://www.apple.com/legal/internet-services/itunes/dev/stdeula/ to the bottom of the app description.
The onboarding has been rewritten. Hopefully clear that a subscription must be purchased to proceed. The screenshots no longer have watermarks. No user login is now required or stored. The purpose strings have been extended to be meaningful and are also explained in more detail during on-boarding. Help has been rewritten.
>
> This app allows users to optionally select a contact from their device's Contacts database to create a Crew record for use in their logbook. The contact information is stored in Core Data and synchronized to the user's private iCloud account via CloudKit. If the user shares a boat with crew, that boat's crew records go to the people they invited, through CloudKit sharing. No contact data is uploaded to any developer-controlled server or shared with any third party.
>
> The App privacy policy has been updated to make this clearer.
>
> We've updated the subscription screen to ensure the billed amount ($year and $/month) is now the most prominent element in the layout. The trial information is now displayed in a subordinate position with less visual emphasis, in line with App Store Guideline 3.1.2.
